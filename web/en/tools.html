ES Download

Nine views, one by one.

One for each job. None of them does two things at once.

01

Summary

Your Mac’s report, with free space, disk health and what can be recovered. The cleanup starts here.

02

Mac status

CPU, memory, disk, network, battery and temperature, live. Pause it and see what is using them.

03

Disk space

The disk map by category, folder and age. Click a color and see what is inside.

04

Large files

The heaviest files on your disk in one table, with their last use. Move them to iCloud or delete them without leaving the list.

05

Duplicates

Copies and near-twins, grouped. Brisa picks which one to keep and explains why.

06

Applications

The app and everything it left behind, by category. macOS apps stay out.

07

History

Every cleanup, what it freed and when. Compare two rounds and clear the log without touching Brisa Trash.

08

Maintenance

Four rules with their own toggle, for the system Trash, forgotten downloads, caches and duplicates. It tells you how much it recovered.

09

Brisa Trash

Everything removed, with its expiry date and its size. Kept for as long as you choose, 7, 30 or 60 days.

Clean your Mac. And undo it if you change your mind.

In one minute you will see how much space you were hiding from yourself, and inside the window you choose, any of it can go back.
