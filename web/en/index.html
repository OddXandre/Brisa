Questions

The questions I get most.

What if it takes something I need?

Cleaning never deletes: what Brisa sets aside waits with its expiry date and comes back within the window —7, 30 or 60 days—. Once that is up, its trash is emptied: then it is gone. Uninstalling is final, and it warns you first.

Does Brisa delete anything on its own?

No. It scans first and shows you the plan: nothing moves until you confirm. The one exception is automatic maintenance, if you turn it on.

Why does macOS warn me the first time I open it?

Because Brisa carries no Apple developer certificate: macOS only lets you open that way what it could not verify. It does not mean anything is wrong with Brisa, and the steps are on the download page.

What does Brisa send over the network?

Nothing, unless you ask it to. The single exception is “Help improve Brisa”, and it sends only what you decide.

What if I do not give it Full Disk Access?

It works all the same: it measures everything else and tells you what is missing. You can withdraw it whenever you want.

How much does it cost?

Nothing: Brisa is free and complete, with no account, no subscription and no paid version.
